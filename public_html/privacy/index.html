Privacy Policy

Translator's Hebrew Bible

Effective Date: May 22, 2026
Service: hebrewbible.dev

1. Information Collection by This Service

The Translator's Hebrew Bible (THB) does not actively collect, track, store, or monitor any personal information, user data, or usage analytics. No cookies are used for tracking purposes, user identification, or data collection.

2. Local Storage Usage

THB uses browser local storage exclusively to save your preferences locally on your device, including:

  • Column visibility settings (which biblical traditions to display)
  • Column order preferences (custom arrangement of text traditions)
  • Font size adjustments for individual text traditions
  • Layout customizations

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any servers. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings or by using the "Reset Layout" function in the application.

3. Web Hosting and Server Logs

THB is hosted by third-party web hosting providers. These hosting providers may maintain standard server logs, access logs, or other technical records as part of their normal operations. We have no control over, access to, or knowledge of what information (if any) may be logged by hosting providers. Any such logging is governed by the hosting provider's own privacy policies and practices, not this Service.

4. Data Usage by This Service

Since THB does not collect any personal data, no personal data is used, analyzed, processed, or shared by this Service for any purpose. All text processing and linguistic analysis occurs locally within your browser.

5. Data Sharing and Sales

THB does not share, sell, rent, or transfer any user information to third parties because no user information is collected or maintained by this Service.

6. Analytics

THB uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to collect anonymized, aggregate traffic data — page views, general geographic region, and device type. No cookies are used, no individuals are tracked, and no personally identifiable information is collected or retained. This data is used solely to understand general usage patterns and improve the service. It is governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

7. User Communications

THB does not maintain systems for user communications, feedback, or support beyond the contact information provided on the website. No contact information is collected or stored by this Service.

8. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or stored by THB, there are no data security risks related to personal information maintained by this Service. Your preference settings are stored locally on your device and protected by your browser's security mechanisms.

9. Data Subject Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Since THB collects no personal data:

  • Right to Access: Not applicable - no personal data exists to access
  • Right to Deletion: Not applicable - no personal data exists to delete
  • Right to Portability: Not applicable - no personal data exists to port
  • Right to Correction: Not applicable - no personal data exists to correct
  • Right to Opt-Out: Not applicable - no data collection to opt out of

10. Children's Privacy

No personal information is collected by THB from users of any age, including children under 13.

11. Browser Storage Management

You can manage or delete your locally stored preferences at any time by:

  • Using the "Reset Layout" button in the THB interface
  • Clearing your browser's local storage for hebrewbible.dev
  • Using your browser's privacy/data management settings

12. Limitation of Control

This privacy policy applies only to THB's own practices. Web hosting providers, internet service providers, and other infrastructure components may have their own data handling practices that are outside our knowledge or control.

13. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Since no user contact information is maintained, users are responsible for periodically reviewing this policy. Continued use of THB constitutes acceptance of any policy changes.

14. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the United States.

Summary: THB collects no personal information and uses no tracking cookies. Layout preferences are saved locally on your device only. Cloudflare Web Analytics provides anonymized, aggregate traffic data (no individuals identified, no cookies). Standard hosting infrastructure may involve additional logging outside our control.
