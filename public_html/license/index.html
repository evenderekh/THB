Usage Guidelines

Academic and Research Use

When citing THB in academic work, please reference both the platform and the underlying source materials as appropriate for your research context:

Platform Citation:
Muzar, M. (2026). Translator's Hebrew Bible (THB) [Digital platform].
https://hebrewbible.dev

Additionally, cite the specific source datasets that your research relies upon, using the attribution information provided above for each tradition.

Redistribution and Modification

The THB platform (MIT License) may be freely modified and redistributed. However, users must respect the licensing terms of the underlying text sources:

GPL 3.0 components (LXX text): Any redistribution containing this material must also be GPL 3.0 compatible.

CC-BY-NC components (DSS): Commercial redistribution of this material is prohibited; non-commercial use requires attribution.

CC-BY components (SP, OpenScriptures morphology, BDB Enhanced enhancements): Requires attribution as specified above.

CC-BY-SA components (LSJ, L&S, Copenhagen Alliance): Requires attribution; derivative works must be released under the same or a compatible license.

CC-BY-NC 4.0 components (OpenHebrewBible KJV alignment): Non-commercial use only; requires attribution to Eliran Wong.

Contact

For questions about licensing, attribution, or data sources, contact the THB project maintainers. For issues with specific source datasets, contact the original data providers directly.
